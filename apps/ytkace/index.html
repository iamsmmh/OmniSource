01 About

YTKACE is a free, open-source YouTube enhancer and downloader for iOS. No ads, background playback, video and audio downloads, SponsorBlock, Picture in Picture, player gestures and deep interface customization.

Release notes
YouTube 21.35.3 | YTKACE 0.9.2 | YTKACE_0.9.2_YouTube_21.35.3

by @itzzace0

What’s new:
• Updated for YouTube 21.35.3
• Added a save button to community post images, saved at full resolution
• Added Posts to the tab bar, so it can be shown as a tab or set as the startup tab
• Added a choice of where downloads are saved, between the YTKACE library, Photos, or asking each time
• Added a custom hold speed with a slider from 0.25x to 5x, and the speed pill now shows the speed you set
• Added a toggle to remove the ⋯ button from the action bar
• Added a toggle to remove the row of buttons under the progress bar in fullscreen
• Added a toggle to limit gestures to landscape only

Fixed:
• Fixed the app freezing while saving large videos to Photos
• Fixed tapping an image in the comments opening the share sheet
• Fixed a crash when rotating the player
• Fixed a crash when leaving the app while a message was on screen

YouTube 21.35.3 requires iOS 17, so there are two IPAs:
• `YTKACE_0.9.2_YouTube_21.35.3.ipa` for iOS 17 and newer
• `YTKACE_0.9.2_YouTube_iOS16_21.33.6.ipa` for iOS 16
