01 About

MAME4iOS brings the Multiple Arcade Machine Emulator to iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, running thousands of classic arcade games from the 70s, 80s, 90s and beyond. Includes an on-screen joystick, full MFi controller support, netplay, and a menu to organize your legally dumped ROM sets.
