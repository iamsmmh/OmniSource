01 About

iNKillerPlus is a modified Instagram by iKarwan. Download posts, reels, stories and DM media, save profile pictures, block promoted posts and ads, toggle seen/unseen for stories and DMs, ghost typing, hide tabs, copy comments and bios, confirmation prompts before liking or calling, and a profile analyzer. iPadOS supported.
