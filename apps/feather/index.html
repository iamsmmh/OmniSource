01 About

Feather is a free on-device iOS and iPadOS application manager built with UIKit. Import your own developer or enterprise certificate to sign and install IPAs directly from the device, add AltStore-compatible repositories to browse and update apps, keep a library of signed and imported apps, and tweak bundle identifiers, display names, icons and injected dylibs per app - no computer or AltServer required.
