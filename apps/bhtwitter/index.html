01 About

BHTwitter is a long-running tweak for X (formerly Twitter), published as a ready-to-sideload IPA. It hides promoted posts and ads, restores the chronological timeline as the default, and lets you download videos, GIFs and images straight from a post. It also adds Face ID / Touch ID app lock, custom fonts, undo posting, inline translation, direct saving of profile pictures and headers, confirmation prompts for likes and reposts, and switches for hiding the tabs and UI elements you never use.
