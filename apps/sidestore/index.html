01 About

SideStore is a fork of AltStore that signs and refreshes sideloaded apps directly on the device. After a one-time pairing-file setup it renews 7-day certificates in the background using your own Apple ID and an anisette server, so apps stop expiring while you are away from a computer. It installs apps from any AltStore-compatible source, keeps them updated, and can back up and restore app data.
