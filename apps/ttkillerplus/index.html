01 About

TTKillerPlus is a modified TikTok by iKarwan. Download videos from the feed, stories and Explore, save photos and profile avatars, merge post photos with their music, block promoted posts and ads, toggle seen/unseen, hide tabs, browse the feed of a specific country, show country flags and post dates, and auto-like in live streams. iPadOS supported.
