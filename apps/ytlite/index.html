01 About

YouTubePlus (formerly YTLite) is a lightweight enhancer for YouTube developed by Dayanch96. It provides complete ad blocking, background playback, offline video and audio downloads, Picture-in-Picture (PiP), SponsorBlock integration, gesture controls, and customizable interface settings.
