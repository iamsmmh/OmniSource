01 About

MaxMusic (formerly YTMUltimate+) is Mark02-2012's fork of Dayanch96's YTMusicUltimate for the YouTube Music app on iOS. It keeps everything YTMusicUltimate provides — no ads, background playback, download manager, audio quality selection — and adds Return YouTube Music Dislike, YouMusicPiP, YTMABConfig and VolumeBoostYT, plus fixed in-app downloads on newer YouTube Music versions. Settings live inside YouTube Music under Settings.
