01 About

MaxTube (formerly YTPlusM) is Mark02-2012's fork of YTPlus/YTLite, the enhancer for the YouTube app on iOS. On top of the YTPlus feature set — video/audio/thumbnail downloads, SponsorBlock, PiP, YTUHD, YouQuality, Return YouTube Dislike, YTABConfig, DontEatMyContent and over 100 configurable options without a subscription — MaxTube integrates ten extra tweaks: YTweaks, Gonerino, YouSpeed, YTLowContrastMode, VolumeBoostYT, YouGetCaption, YTPlaybackFix, YouChooseQuality, YouShare and PleaseDontStopTheMusic. Settings live inside YouTube under Settings.
